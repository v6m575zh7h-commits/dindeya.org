ONE VILLAGE. A WORLDWIDE FAMILY.

Our roots run deep.
Our reach goes further.

Dindeya is a historic village in Guinea’s Mamou region, shaped by Fulani culture, Islamic heritage, and a deep sense of belonging.

Today, our community stretches from Guinea to the United States, Canada, Europe, and Japan. The Dindeya Development & Support Org. Inc. brings that community together to invest in the place we call home.
